Fixed in commit `a6f2719` (branch `agent/doc-perf-round`).

**Root cause.** The path in `TAG_REGULAR_SHAPE_PHASE_2` is an edge *template*: one edge, usually a two-point line at (-576pt, -576pt). It is not the shape's outline. The importer stored it as `QuickShape.path`, so every quick shape got sliver bounds far from where it sat and was culled. The importer also dropped the circular flag, the stellation offset and both curvatures.

**Fix.** Added `xarast_geom::regular_shape_outline`, which builds polygons, stars, ellipses, rounded corners and one-cubic edge templates. The facts it follows are now documented in `research/01 §4.7.1`. The importer generates the outline in the shape's own space and then applies the record's matrix. `QuickShape` now keeps all of its parameters and has `outline()`. The bounds fallback now centres the axes on the shape. Regression tests use synthetic data only: `import::tests::a_quick_shape_gets_its_generated_outline_not_its_edge_template`, `a_circular_quick_shape_is_an_ellipse_spanning_its_axes`, and 5 geometry tests, including hostile parameters. `fuzz_xar_import` ran for 5 minutes (3.0M execs) with no findings.

**Result.** RedStar renders a six-pointed star (44% ink), Test00 a blue rectangle, and TestBitmapFill its outline. Over the corpus, 42 files have primitives and all 42 paint (before: 38 with primitives, 35 painting). The bitmap fill itself is still not painted; that belongs to the renderer.
