Fixed by XARA-T-0037 (`65f00f1` importer, `417cca3` gradient mapping). Neither the transparency stack nor the blend family was at fault. There were two causes: the black current fill had been taken for the default fill, and every gradient wrapped.

Verified:
- **Headless.** `xarast-cli render Designs/SimpleSphere.xar` now shows the sphere, the rings and the binary text; ink went from black to 76 %.
- **Real window.** `xarast --screenshot` on the COSMIC Wayland session renders the same. The window closed itself after the capture.

The pin is a pixel probe, `crates/xarast-app/tests/fidelity.rs::an_unfilled_frame_does_not_take_the_current_fill`: a synthetic `.xar` holding an unfilled frame over a gradient, under a black current fill. No corpus bytes are involved.
