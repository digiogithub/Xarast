Done in `28a370f`. `ViewTransform` now has `y_up`. The y mappings and the vertical ruler honour it. `document_view` copies the orientation from the viewport and no longer negates y: the page is `(lo.x, hi.y, hi.x, lo.y)`.
`the_interface_page_edge_lands_on_the_rendered_page` still passes. New tests: `the_vertical_ruler_reads_document_y_over_the_page`, which checks ticks over the page against its y range, the top edge reading `hi.y` at 1.25×, and that ticks fall down the screen. Also new: y-up round-trip, zoom-anchor and ruler tests. A real-window screenshot of BLUECAR shows the ruler reading 1450 at the top down to 300.
I also fixed a latent bug the new tests found: minor ruler ticks with a negative index carried the wrong value, because truncating `/` was mixed with `rem_euclid`.
