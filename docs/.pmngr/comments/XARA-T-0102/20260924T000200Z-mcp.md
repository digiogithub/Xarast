Partly absorbed by XARA-US-0043 as XARA-T-0258: conical, diamond (non-perspective), three- and four-colour (parallelogram) fills and conical/diamond transparencies are now baked into SVG **geometry** (`crates/xarast-format/src/svg/bake.rs`, marked `xarast:generated="fill-bake"`, twins unchanged; a baked diamond gained an `<xarast:fill type="diamond">` twin the reader reads). Corpus SVG export vs our PNG: Fill Types simple 19.7 → 1.9/255, WATCH2 16.5 → 0.7, WATCH 5.1 → 2.4; their SVG limits are gone.

Still open here: the `BakeProvider` trait, filter baking (fractal/noise via feTurbulence, feathers), raster baking (perspective gradients, projective four-colour fills), live-effect regeneration, and mesh transparencies (they follow the renderer, which draws them flat: XARA-T-0256).
