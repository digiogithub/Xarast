Root cause: the same as XARA-T-0154. scope3 simple's two wood-tile bitmaps (Wood1.tif, Redtile8.tif) are tag-71 JPEG8BPP with 256-entry palettes; after a round trip they decoded as plain JPEG, hence ≤ 4 levels on 9 % of pixels. Fixed by d458c54; the render round trip is pixel-identical for 59/59 and KNOWN_RENDER_GAPS is deleted.
