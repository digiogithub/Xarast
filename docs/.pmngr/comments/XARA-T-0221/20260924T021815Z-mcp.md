## Report (branch worktree-agent-af4d6a50a5bcaed38, 6 commits on 5166226)

**What was built.** The dirty region comes from comparing scenes, on the render thread, and it covers every edit, not only fill edits.
- `xarast_render::scene_damage` (`damage.rs`) compares the scene tree on screen with the new one. It matches the common prefix and suffix, then matches the rest by key and op equality, keeping the longest order-preserving run. Ramps and images are compared by content, because an evicted ramp slot gets reused. The result is the device bounds of every unmatched leaf on either side, merged into at most 4 rectangles.
- `reuse::plan` → `Plan::Repaint` / `FrameReuse::Repainted`. This happens when a new scene arrives at the kept frame's exact view: only the damage is rasterised over the kept pixels, with `fresh` set to the damage and `base` to the kept generation. For a Final, the kept frame's Draft rectangle (`Kept::inexact`) is repainted as well, so after a fill drag the Final redraws only the dragged area. A frame is drawn whole when the damage covers more than half the view, or when the view moved.
- The shell's `TilePlanner` keeps the tiles of that level under the view and uploads only `fresh` (`TileStore::retain`, `GpuTileCache::retain`).
- The session double-buffers its scene, because the worker keeps the scene of the frame on screen.
- **Exact pixels:** the CPU rasteriser now starts coverage at the primitive's left edge and at its band's top, not at the corner of the draw area. Before this, a sub-rectangle moved `aa_edge_45` by 1/255 in up to 53 pixels. One-call renders and all goldens are unchanged. The scroll and column tests in `render_thread.rs` used to allow a difference of 1/255 and now require exact equality.

**Acceptance.** A fill edit repaints the object's device bounds, not the viewport. Tests: `render_thread::an_edit_repaints_its_damage_and_matches_a_full_render`, where undo repaints the same rectangles back to the first frame byte for byte, and `edit_damage::a_fill_edit_repaints_the_object_bounds`.

**Correctness tests.**
- `properties::repainting_the_damage_gives_the_new_frame`: 512 random scenes with layers, clips and blend families, each given random edits. The repainted frame must equal the new frame byte for byte. The test was mutation-checked.
- `xarast-shell/tests/edit_damage.rs` over all 59 corpus files. Edits: fill recolours, moves, deletions, undo and redo, and fill-tool drags checked in flight at Draft; about a third of the edits are drawn Draft before the Final. Every frame and the tile composite must equal a full render byte for byte.
- `determinism::coverage_does_not_depend_on_the_draw_area`.

**Measured.** Over the corpus: 817 frames, 512 of them repainted. The repainted frames rasterised 1.57 M pixels against 39.3 M for the same frames drawn whole, which is **4.0 % (a 96 % saving)**. The tile uploads were the same 1.57 M pixels. The exact-coverage change costs about 1 ms on the zoomed `pan_draft` (10.4 → 11.4 ms) and 5–9 % on the zoomed Final at rest. Details are in `perf.md`.
