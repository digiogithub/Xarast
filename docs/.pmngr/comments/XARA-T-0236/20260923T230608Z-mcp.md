This is done under XARA-US-0060 (commit 9652549). `cargo xtask export-check` renders every exported SVG with resvg at the reference PNG's size and fails on `xarast:`, on a parse failure, or when the mean |Δ| goes over the limit: 4/255 by default, with per-file limits and their reasons in `xtask/export-limits-{fixtures,corpus}.txt`. CI runs `svg-check --interchange` and `export-check --require-tools` over the built-in fixtures on every push (job `export`). The nightly `export-corpus.yml` runs them over the 59-file corpus. Corpus SVG median: 0.73/255.
