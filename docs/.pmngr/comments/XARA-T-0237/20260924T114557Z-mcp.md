**How it works:** `DeleteText`, `CutText`, and a `TypeText`/`PasteText` that replaces a range with nothing now call `xarast_doc::remove_empty_story` inside their own transaction. The story is removed when all it holds is its final break. So the removal is part of the step that emptied the story and merges into the Backspace burst: one undo restores the story and its text, and the digest matches exactly. The original gets the same result another way: its `OpDeleteTextStory` merges into the previous operation.

**Edge-case decisions** (text.md "Removing an emptied story"; tools.md decision 70):
- **Caret after removal:** a pending caret at the story's origin (the translation part of its matrix). A column story keeps its width; rotation and shear are lost. The caret carries the removed text's attributes as its pending style, so typing again recreates a story that looks the same.
- **Leaving the tool** (Esc, a tool switch, a click elsewhere) removes nothing. This keeps invariant 11: leaving is not an edit. It differs from the original in one case only: a story holding nothing but paragraph breaks (typed Enter) stays.
- **"Empty"** means nothing but the final break. That is stricter than the original's "no TextChar". A deletion of nothing never removes a story, so an imported empty story stays.
- **Text on a path:** the path stays where the story was, as an ordinary shape. It keeps the non-text attributes it was painted with (the story's included, same rule as Convert to shapes). Editing ends with no caret, and the path is not selected (the original deselects it).
- **Selection:** the session's prune drops the removed story, so nothing is selected.
