Notes from XARA-US-0018 (2026-09-24). I used scratch scripts only; nothing was committed.

- **Preview format:** 36 of 37 Designs/testfiles files carry a GIF87a preview (tag 61) before the compressed section. They are palettised, and 47–128 px on the long side.
- **Framing:** the preview frame is not always our "drawing" frame. Fill Types simple matches `--area drawing` exactly. Watch4 does not: our drawing bounds come out at 122×54 against the preview's 122×98.
- **Registration that worked:**
  1. Render `--area page` at 60 dpi and pad it with white.
  2. Search the crop (x0, y0, scale) that, box-filtered to the preview's size, minimises mean |Δ|. Use a coarse grid on a 3×-preview copy, then coordinate descent on the full render.
- **Mean |Δ| after registration:** GardenPlan 5.0, BLUECAR 6.1, Watch4 13.0, scope3 20.0, WATCH2 20.9, leafgirl 23.1, WATCH 30.6, Groucho2 44.3, SoftShadow 73.1. The worst cases come from live effects, bevels and text.
- **Where the method lives:** `docs/memory/render.md`, section "Blend families against the original's previews". It worked for per-family counterfactuals and settled mesh tiling (XARA-T-0256).
