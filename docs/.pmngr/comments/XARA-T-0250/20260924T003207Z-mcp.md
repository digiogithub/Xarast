Done on branch `worktree-agent-a675e321d193b6b59` (worktree agent-a054a11e5d4fc0ca5), based on 0b7f21a:
- bc1c2c5 `core: follow the fitted text with carets, hits and highlights on a path`
- 86824b6 `docs: record carets, hits and selection on a path`

What changed: the text tool lays stories out with `text::lay_story`, the same function the walker uses, and builds `CaretMap::on_path(text, layout, fit)`. Each stop now records which cluster it belongs to (`Stop::cluster`). `caret_segments` draws the caret at that cluster's fitted edge, turned (and sheared) with the glyph. `hit_point` returns the nearest fitted cluster box; where boxes overlap it takes the one the point is deepest inside, and it also returns the distance, which the tool uses to decide whether a click is on the story. `selection_quads` gives one quad per cluster. A long manual kern is cut into pieces that follow the path. Caret movement is unchanged: it still happens in the straight layout. No changes to the ui or shell were needed.

Acceptance, checked in `xarast-app/tests/text_path_caret.rs` (6 tests, 5 of them on TextCurve.xar):
- Every stop is within 1 mp of its fitted cluster edge, lies on the glyph's baseline and is parallel to the glyph's vertical. Over 1000 stops are checked, and most of them are turned.
- Clicking each glyph at 20% and at 80% of its width gives the nearer edge.
- Every stop survives a round trip through a click.
- Pressing Right visits every boundary in logical order.
- Through Session: a click, Right, and a three-character selection drawn as three turned quads.
- A synthetic arc test covers the same ground without the corpus.

Gates: fmt ok; clippy -D warnings ok; workspace tests 1710 passed, 0 failed, 1 ignored (with the corpus); cargo deny ok; build --locked ok.

Limitation: where the path bends hard and tracking is tight, neighbouring fitted boxes overlap near their shared edge, so a click right at the edge may give the other side of the same offset. Details are in text.md under "Carets on a path".
