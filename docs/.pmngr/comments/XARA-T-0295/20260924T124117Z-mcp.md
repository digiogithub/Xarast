## Report (implementation ready for review)

Branch `worktree-agent-ae31937ea4f2d32a3`, on top of 8269a5e:
- `b307457` core: reap only detached roots when dropping the redo branch
- `e8f5408` core: remove a text story of paragraph breaks when editing ends
- `287ef2d` docs: record removing a story of breaks on leaving the text

**Decisions**
- **Empty means no characters** (`xarast_doc::holds_no_characters`): the text holds only `\n` and `\t`. Tabs do not count, following the original (it looks for a `TextChar`, and a tab is not one). Soft breaks and kerns are not part of the text. A space counts as a character.
- **Deletion:** `DeleteText`, `CutText`, and a `TypeText`/`PasteText` that inserts nothing remove the story in the same transaction once only breaks are left. Typing that inserts something, such as Enter over all the text, never removes the story at the command; it goes when editing ends.
- **Leaving:** every exit (Esc, a tool switch, a click on the canvas or into another story) goes through `TextTool::set`, which calls `leave`. `leave` emits the new `EditCommand::RemoveEmptyStory { story, joins }` ("Delete Text"), but only if the tool edited that story since the caret went in (`edited`) and the story is still reachable, editable and empty.
- **Merge:** `joins` is the `CoalesceKey` of the tool's last burst on that story. The history merges the removal only while that burst is still the last step. Otherwise, for example after the burst was undone or a paste or attribute edit came last, the removal is its own "Delete Text" step. The original merges unconditionally; we don't, so that one undo never takes back more than the user did.
- **Imported empty stories** that were only entered are never touched.
- **On a path**, the path stays behind as a shape (T-0237 code, unchanged).
- **Pre-existing history bug, fixed:** `reap` destroyed nodes that a dropped redo branch retained, even when they sat inside a subtree the new commit had just deleted. A story deleted after an undone Enter came back from undo without its final line. It affected any object Delete too. `reap` now destroys only detached roots. This is recorded in document-model.md.
- **Docs:** tools.md invariant 11 (the exception) and decision 70, text.md "Removing an emptied story", document-model.md.

**Tests** (in `tests/text_tool.rs`, plus `xarast-doc` unit tests):
- Enter×3 then Esc leaves one step, and one undo gives the digest from before.
- A tool switch, a click on the canvas and a click into another story each remove a story holding a tab and breaks.
- The fallback case: the removal is its own step.
- Deleting every character but the breaks removes the story in that step.
- Enter over all the text keeps the story until Esc.
- An imported `"\n"` or `"\n\n"` story survives being entered and left.
- The repaint after leaving equals a full render.
- Regression tests for the `reap` bug, at doc level and at session level.
