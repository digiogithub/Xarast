## Report (branch worktree-agent-a6f817c2a1c18b088, 5 commits 2b214ee..9352d54 on 7ce0197)

**Built**
- `xarast_app::photo_panel`: `PhotoPanelView` (the one selected bitmap object, its chain, editable, dragging) and `PhotoPanelOp::{Preview, Commit, Cancel, Set, Reset}`, carried by `Intent::PhotoPanel`. A slider drag follows the fill-drag pattern (tools.md decision 45, now decisions 74 and 75). `Preview` stores `(node, chain)` in `Preview::photo`. The document is not touched until `Commit`, which makes one `set_photo_ops` call and so one "Adjust Photo" step. `Cancel`, `Esc` (which the drag consumes, so the selection stays), undo and redo drop the preview. A crop or orientation change is applied at once as a `Set`. A chain with an unknown operation is never previewed or set.
- Walker proxy: a previewed chain is evaluated on the smallest pyramid level of the master that still covers the object on screen, capped at `PROXY_MAX_PIXELS` = 2.1 M. The crop is scaled onto the level. One registry slot per previewed object is reused between frames and parked when the drag ends. The node's fingerprint mixes in the chain hash. `DecodedImages` is not touched during a drag.
- `xarast_image::photo::evaluate` now runs its pixel pass (table + mixes) in bands on scoped threads. The output bytes are identical.
- UI `panels::photo` ("Photo"): the chain as a list; Brightness, Contrast, Saturation and Gamma sliders; a Greyscale checkbox; levels per channel; rotate and flip buttons; crop fields; Remove crop; Reset all. An unknown operation gives a read-only "Non-editable: unknown operation ‘kind’" state. The panel docks as a second tab in the bitmap gallery's cell (new `UiHost::set_default_layout_grouped`). Dock tabs are now published to AccessKit as `Role::Tab` with the panel's name.
- Found in egui 0.33: pressing `Esc` during a slider drag ends the drag and reports `drag_stopped` in the same frame. The photo panel treats that frame as a cancel. The colour editor probably commits there instead: XARA-T-0305.

**Tests**: `xarast-app/tests/photo_panel.rs` (5), `xarast-ui/tests/photo_panel.rs` (7), 2 walker unit tests, 1 op-label unit test, 1 panel unit test, 1 shell mapping test.

- `Esc` mid-drag restores the digest, history, undo label, chain and pixels.

- Every control is named. Brightness and Rotate right are reached with Tab and operated with the keyboard. A pointer drag sends previews and then exactly one Commit. `Esc` sends Cancel.
