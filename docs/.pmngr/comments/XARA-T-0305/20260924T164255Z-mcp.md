Report (branch worktree-agent-a17f848f02f0e6503, commits d096bf4, 389d10c, c3fae04, docs 718f02f).

**Confirmed by kittest before the fix** (`crates/xarast-ui/tests/colour_editor.rs`): Esc during a drag of a colour editor number field, Tint slider or the 2D field/strip sent `Commit` and never `Cancel`. egui 0.33 drops the drag *and* the press in the Esc frame. So `classify` sees `drag_stopped` and `PressState::track` sees the button "released". The note guessed that the field and strip were unaffected; they were affected too.

**Fixed, the photo panel's way:** a release in a frame where Esc was pressed now counts as `Cancel` in `ColourPanel::classify` and `PressState::track`. `classify` also gained `LiveCommit`, as the photo panel has.

**Also found and fixed:**
- The tint and shade sliders sent a `Set` every frame. `egui::Slider` with `fixed_decimals` rounds a stored 0.4 (40.000000596 %) every frame and reports it as a change. The photo panel had the same bug with Brightness 0.2 and Gamma 1.3. Both panels now hand the slider an already rounded value (`panels::slider_value`).
- Photo crop field: a drag ended by Esc applied the crop. It now applies nothing.
- Canvas: when Esc ended the drag, a text ruler marker drag made its edit and a guide pulled out of a ruler was added. Now neither happens. A guide dragged along the canvas is now put back with one `MoveGuide` to where it started.
- Guides were picked where the pointer was on `drag_started`, which is past egui's 6 pt threshold and outside the 4 pt grab. They are now picked at `press_origin`.

**Checked, no change:** the fill tool's `Real` and `Number` infobar sliders have no preview/commit split. Each change is its own `InfobarEdit`, so Esc commits nothing extra, but it can't undo what was already applied. That needs one gesture per drag first (XARA-T-0220). The text infobar has only text fields and combos. The colour bar, gallery and bitmap drags already keep their own state and have Esc tests.

**Tests:** colour_editor.rs 5 (new); photo_panel.rs +2; text_ruler +1; canvas +1; panels::slider_value +1.
