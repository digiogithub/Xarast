**The premise was wrong.** The keyholes come *first*, not last. `ndclpcnt.h:123-133` and every keyhole walk (`ndclpcnt.cpp:328-339`, `:1938-2000`) put the keyholes **before** the `NodeClipView`. They are also **painted** as ordinary objects, and the clip is the **union** of their filled areas with the outlines stripped (`ClipPathToPath` style 7 = OR). research/01 §4.8 now records this, and research/02 §6.11 is corrected.

**Mapping.** A controller becomes `Group { attrs, keyholes (painted), ClipView(Inside) { Path = union of keyholes (unpainted), clipped… } }`.
- A single path or quick shape under the group's winding rule is copied verbatim.
- Anything else goes through `self_union` and then `boolean(Union)`: several keyholes, a keyhole with its own winding rule, groups, bitmap parallelograms.
- 4084/4085 now have decoders, so the atomic list that real files declare no longer strips them. 4137 is never written, so it stays skipped.
- A new `DiagCode::ClipViewDegraded` reports the degenerate cases: no marker (drawn as a group), a stray marker, a keyhole with no outline, no keyhole outline at all (drawn unclipped), and a point cap of 200k.

**Evidence.** `crates/xarast-app/tests/xar_clips_and_masks.rs` checks these with pixel probes on a synthetic `.xar`:
- red is clipped inside the keyhole;
- the green keyhole is painted beneath;
- white outside the keyhole;
- two keyholes clip to their union;
- a controller with no marker draws as a group;
- the ClipView tags are declared atomic, and nothing is stripped.

`import.rs` tests cover:
- the structure and the verbatim clip;
- the area of an even-odd union;
- every degenerate case, with the record accounting balanced.

The fill census (`fills.rs`) still matches with the corpus. The new fuzz seeds are `clip-view` and `bitmap-transparency`. `fuzz_xar_import` ran 1.46M executions in 4 minutes with no findings.
