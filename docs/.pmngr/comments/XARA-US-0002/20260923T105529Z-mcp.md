Implemented in `0988c75` (xarast-ui) and `b973e27` (xarast-shell).
`xarast_shell::egui_input::EguiInput` builds egui `RawInput` from `ShellEvent` only (no winit): pointer, buttons, wheel, keys and text, Ctrl+C/X/V as egui clipboard events, IME, focus, pinch. The viewer carries egui output back: clipboard, IME enable and caret area, and cursor shape through the new `ShellCtx::set_cursor`.
**Decision on the double-wheel caveat:** the `IntentAdapter` is the only thing that navigates the canvas. The canvas widget runs with `CanvasNavigation::External`, so it emits no Pan or Zoom. The adapter skips presses and wheels that land on an egui layer above the canvas (a popup or window), and view keys are ignored while a text field has the keyboard. egui also smooths one wheel notch over about 11 frames, which is one more reason its path can't be the owner.
Evidence: headless end-to-end tests run through real egui frames: one Ctrl+wheel notch zooms exactly √2, a wheel pans once, a wheel over the dock doesn't move the document, clicking "Hide layer" hides the layer, and typing "1" in the rename field doesn't zoom. Mutation checks: Internal navigation zooms 1.466 per notch, and removing the text-field gate lets "1" zoom. Both fail the tests. A real-window `--screenshot` run exits 0. Live mouse and keyboard were not injected, to stay out of the maintainer's desktop.
