## Validation on a real compositor (via XARA-US-0012)

Measured in an isolated GNOME 46 / mutter 46.2 session (Vulkan, NVIDIA) with a private bus and xdg-desktop-portal-gtk; COSMIC was measured in US-0001.

Validated as written:
- Input pipeline: pointer enter/move/press/release positions exact, keys with text, modifiers, focus, per-frame stroke drain; resize (maximise, unmaximise, 60-step live border drag, no validation error); minimise (no 0×0 on Wayland, loop idle at ~0 % CPU, clean restore).
- Fractional scale plumbing at 1.25 / 1.5 / 2.0 (sizes and scale always paired); CSD via sctk-adwaita.
- Cursor shapes (12 shapes) and the IME caret area (ibus candidate window under the caret at 1× and 1.25×).
- Settings portal colour scheme answer; clipboard text and RGBA image round trips with GTK peers (through XWayland on GNOME).

Found broken and fixed:
- Portal dialogs (354c59c): rfd turned failures into "cancelled" and spawned zenity; quitting with a dialog open hung; tests could open dialogs on a developer's desktop. Now ashpd FileChooser with correct Cancelled/Failed, a non-blocking shutdown, and `PortalService::offline` for tests.
- Drag and drop (ea25248): winit 0.30 has none on Wayland; the shell now binds `wl_data_device` itself (positions and multi-file drops included).
- Clipboard persistence claim (1de398c): it was about focus loss; the real variable is exit.

Still not validated: KDE and sway (not installed), tablet axes (US-0013), trackpad gestures (no winit 0.30 support), the 8-hour soak (30 min done: no leak). Also found: panel text shrinks every frame in xarast-ui (XARA-T-0046).
