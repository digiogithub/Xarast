**2/5 — W0 spike re-run: G1 and G2 settled** (`cargo bench -p xarast-render --bench spike [--features spike-gpu]`, new harness `benches/spike.rs`). `bulk` = 100 000 twelve-segment polygons at 1920×1080. Each figure is the median of 30–50 frames, and the ranges span 3–5 runs.

**G1 — `vello_cpu` 0.2, ≤ 120 ms single-thread and ≤ 25 ms on 8 cores → FAILS (settled).**

| scene | 1 thread | 8 threads (7 workers + 1) | 24 threads |
|---|---|---|---|
| heavy r3..28 px (the recorded gate scene, ≈×27 overdraw) | **159–164 ms** (record 104 + raster 56) | **53–58 ms** (55.6 pinned to the P-cores) | 49–51 ms |
| light r2..8 px (≈×2.6) | 83–88 ms | 34–37 ms | 35–39 ms |

Parallel scaling stops at about 3×. Going from 8 to 24 threads buys almost nothing, because recording is serial. More cores will not close G1.

**G2 — `vello` 0.10, ≤ 8 ms → FAILS on the integrated GPU the gate names, and sits right at the line on the discrete GPU.** The timer runs from submit until `poll(wait)` returns, with the scene retained. That is an upper bound.

| adapter | heavy | light | scene encode (CPU, once per scene change) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intel Arrow Lake iGPU (Mesa 26.1.6) | **72–86 ms** (197 at load 34) | 32–48 ms | 17–25 ms |
| NVIDIA RTX 4000 SFF Ada | **7.8–8.0 ms** (9.3 at load 34) | 5.8–6.0 ms | 17–25 ms |

The output agrees with `vello_cpu`: mean absolute channel difference 0.019 (heavy) and 0.67 (light, AA edges).

Candidate C `vello_hybrid` 0.2: its GPU part is fast (5.5 ms light on the RTX), but its CPU strip recording costs 63–107 ms per frame, the same as `vello_cpu`, so it is no escape. It also showed a 2.5 s outlier on the RTX in one run and a 1.77 mean diff on the Intel iGPU.

**Finding:** `vello` 0.10 and `vello_hybrid` 0.2 both pin **wgpu 29**, while the workspace is on wgpu 30. Task R5.1 has to reconcile the two.
