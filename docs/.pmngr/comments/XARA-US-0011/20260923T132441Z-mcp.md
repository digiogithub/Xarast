XARA-T-0050 is done. The GPU tile compositor is wired into the viewer and the capability ladder works: adapters are tried in the order WGPU_ADAPTER_NAME, then high-performance, then the software fallback. The canvas runs on GPU tiles and falls back to the CPU tier through `compose_cpu` when XARAST_RENDERER=cpu, when the cache cannot be created, or when GPU errors persist. The status bar shows the tier. "Degrades gracefully to CPU" was checked live with injected GPU errors, lavapipe and GL. Pan/zoom at 100k on the iGPU has p99 ≤ 9.6 ms even at 4K (XARA-T-0008 is done). The story stays in_review for the maintainer.
