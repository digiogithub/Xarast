Round report, 2026-09-24. Branch `worktree-agent-a2af95ba6ff41af94`, commits 46213c8, d90565b, 0d14513, d961a78, 4e57720.

**The acceptance criteria are NOT met.** R4.4 (luminance weights), R4.5 (the `CalcTransparencyX` tables), verifying Contrast/Bevel/Saturation/Luminosity, and the 25/100/400 % comparison all need the original running on an x86-64 VM. There is no VM here.

**What could be checked:** each corpus file embeds a preview that the original rendered: a GIF, 256 colours, at most 128 px. For each file I registered our render against its preview, with a mean |Δ| of 5.0 to 44 after registration. Then, for every transparency mode the file uses, I redrew that mode's objects in each of the other 10 families and in None, and scored every variant over the pixels those objects affect. Details are in `docs/memory/render.md`, section "Blend families against the original's previews".

- **Stained Glass:** our family scores best of 11 in all 6 files where it matters.
  - Watch4: 27.9 vs Darken 32.7 and Mix 33.6.
  - Groucho2: 51.0 vs Mix 52.6.
  - scope3: 20.8 vs Darken 21.0.
  - BLUECAR: 14.2 vs Mix 17.0.
  - leafgirl: 28.6 vs Mix 33.7.
  - WATCH: 26.8 vs Mix 27.5.
- **Bleach:** best in scope3 (20.6, tied with Lighten; Mix 30.1) and BLUECAR (21.2, tied with Lighten).
  - Watch4 (2nd) and SoftShadow (6th) do not count as evidence: those regions are dominated by features we do not render yet.
- **Darken:** inconclusive. It appears only in Watch4, on 38 px.
- **Brightness:** no pixel at preview size depends on it.
- **Not in any corpus file:** Contrast, Saturation, Lighten, Luminosity and Hue. These are exactly the reconstructed families, so the previews cannot verify them.
- **Luminance weights:** not recoverable from 256-colour GIFs this small.
- **Mismatches found and fixed:**
  - A Hue transparency (byte 31, `TT_HUE`) was read as Mix. `TranspMode` has a Hue variant now, and the tool offers it as the original does.
  - Mesh transparencies were drawn flat in Mix. They are now per pixel and in their own family, and meshes tile mirrored (XARA-T-0256).
- **Filed:** XARA-T-0307. Bitmap transparencies lose their levels and their mode on import, so they render in Mix.

The story stays VM-gated for R4.4, R4.5 and the four reconstructed families.
