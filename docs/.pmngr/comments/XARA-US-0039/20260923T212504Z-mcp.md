**Criteria met**

- T8.4.3/T8.4.4: the infobar has these fields: type (mutates the fill), effect or blend mode (9 modes), tiling (Simple/Repeating), ramp mapping, bias and gain sliders, stop position, and stop transparency. They use the new `InfobarItem::Choice` and `InfobarItem::Real` widgets.

- `EditCommand::Fill` carries the xarast-doc fill commands. This is the fill half of XARA-T-0212.

**Design change from the phase text:** drags do not emit a command on every frame. The tool shows the change through `Preview::attrs` and commits one step on release, so Esc leaves nothing behind. The reasons are in tools.md decision 45.

  - one undo step per drag, and undo restores the exact state;

  - Esc leaves the digest and the history length unchanged.
