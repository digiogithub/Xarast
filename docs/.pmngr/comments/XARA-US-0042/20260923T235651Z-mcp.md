## Report (branch worktree-agent-a675e321d193b6b59)

Done:
- T8.7.1: colour bar under the canvas (`xarast-ui/src/colour_bar.rs`). It is one painted strip with one interaction per visible swatch. It has paging (buttons and the wheel) and a menu (New, Edit, Move left/right, Delete). The first swatch is "no colour", then the named colours in line order, then 14 standard colours. Every swatch is an AccessKit button with its name and its `#RRGGBB` value.
- T8.7.2: left click sets the fill; right click or Shift+click sets the line. As in the original, a gradient is flattened, unless the fill tool has a stop selected, in which case only that stop changes. With nothing selected, the click sets the current attribute.
- T8.7.3: in-window colour drag, with nothing written to the document before the drop. Every move resolves a target. The pointer shows Copy or NoDrop, and the status line says what a drop would do. Esc cancels.
- T8.7.4: drop targets are a stop, the arm (adds a stop), the outline with Shift (line), the interior (fill, flattening a gradient, including an interior with no colour), and a palette slot (named colour: reorder; direct colour: redefine a plain named colour). Criterion 10 is tested.
- T8.7.5: colour gallery panel with a derivation tree, New, Edit, Rename and Delete (Detach), and draggable swatches.
- Colour-line order uses `entry_index` (`ColourTable::listed/move_listed`, `MoveColour`).
- XARA-T-0247: the colour editor edits the fill tool's selected stop, and its title names the stop.
- Bench: about 6 µs per drag move at 100k objects (budget 1 ms). It reuses the phase-7 pick index, so no new spatial index was needed.

Tests: `xarast-app/tests/colour_bar.rs` (13), plus unit, accessibility and shell tests.

Filed: XARA-T-0253 (eyedropper), XARA-T-0254 (F9), XARA-T-0255 (transparency/stroke handle drops, "Set Fill" label on line changes, delete confirmation). XARA-T-0204 (first-class "no colour") is still open; the bar uses the importer's transparent flat colour.
