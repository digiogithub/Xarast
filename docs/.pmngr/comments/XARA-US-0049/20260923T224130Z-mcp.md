**What was built**
- T9.6.3 (XARA-T-0243): Ctrl+Shift+C "Convert to Editable Shapes" now converts text stories too. `xarast_app::convert::ConvertCommand` lays each story out with the walker's own geometry (`text::story_outlines` = `build_story`), and `xarast_doc::convert_story_to_shapes` swaps the story for a group in the same z-position. The group holds one path per attribute run. Each path's own attributes are only the non-text slots that differ from what the story inherits, with winding forced to non-zero. Slotless attributes such as the object name move to the group. Selecting a group converts every shape and story inside it. Selecting a story leaves its new group selected. A selection with nothing convertible records no undo step. A story with no ink is left alone.
- T9.6.4 (XARA-T-0244): the group keeps the story's text in `GroupNode::source_text`. In `.xarast` it is written as `xarast:was-text="true"` plus `<xarast:text-source>`, and read back. It is also in the digest (appended only when present), the dump and the normal form.
