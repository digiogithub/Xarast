**What was built (XARA-T-0270):**
- One sampler for image fills, placed bitmaps and bitmap transparencies. `TranspSource::Image` now has a `Filter`.
- Filtering is premultiplied: transparent texels no longer bleed their colour.
- Magnification uses Mitchell–Netravali at HighQuality and bilinear at Bilinear.
- From 1× to 2× minification, a tent widened by the footprint. From 2×, trilinear over a lazily built linear-light mip pyramid.
- The level of detail comes from the mapping's Jacobian: once per primitive for affine mappings, per pixel for perspective.
- Aligned 1:1 fast path (T10.3.7): every filter samples as a point, pinned bit for bit.
- Contone is applied per texel through a 256-entry table.
- `draw_image_cmd` now readies its samplers once per command instead of once per pixel. Aligned case 3.86 → 0.83 ms per 512² frame.

**Measured decisions** (the numbers are in render.md):
- Mitchell has the least ringing of the cubic candidates and still reconstructs better than bilinear.
- **Deviation from the phase document:** magnification averages in encoded sRGB, not linear light. Encoded won on all 16 image × ratio rows, rang less, and avoided a visible light leak in a magnified tiny bitmap (`bitmap_orientation.rs` caught it).
- Trilinear lost to the widened tent at ÷1.5 on all four test images.
