## Report (worktree branch `worktree-agent-a9e197e8c43e9901b`, on top of 0433a76)

Commits: f6974d5 render (budget, spill, proxy), 0da5df4 render (benches), 3b92906 core (walker sources + prepare), 12911d4 docs (render.md, image.md, perf.md).

**What was built.** It lives in `xarast-render` (`pixel_budget.rs`, `spill.rs`), not `xarast-image`: the levels being budgeted are `ImageRef`'s. `ImageRef` is now a handle to a shared store of levels: the base plus the mip pyramid, each one resident or evicted. Samplers pin the levels they need once per primitive, so eviction never pulls pixels from under a draw. Levels at or below the proxy are never evicted. The proxy starts as the largest level with a long edge ≤ 256, grows to the drawn level, and is capped at 2048. The larger levels are evicted least recently drawn first when over the limit. The limit comes from `XARAST_PIXEL_BUDGET_MB`, or else ¼ of RAM clamped to 512 MiB–4 GiB. Reduced levels come back through the same reduction. A base comes back from its spill file, or else from its `PixelSource`. The walker gives native pixels a cheap copy source that is never spilled. It gives encoded originals a re-decode source; their bases are spilled once, the first time they are evicted. There is one spill directory per session, under `$XDG_CACHE_HOME/xarast/spill`, locked for its lifetime. A stale directory from a crashed session is swept on the next start. The walker now builds pyramids on its decode threads (T10.5.2), not the render thread.

**Byte-for-byte proof** (in the default gate):
- `xarast-render/tests/pixel_budget.rs`: every image case of the feature corpus, under a limit-0 budget with every level above 1×1 evictable. It covers spill, expensive-source and cheap-source re-materialisation, lazy and prepared pyramids, and deterministic and parallel rendering, and asserts identical bytes. It also tests the proxy, the cap, accounting, spill-file lifetime and equality.
- `xarast-app/tests/pixel_budget.rs`: all 24 corpus files with bitmaps, Groucho2 and leafgirl included, at Final and Draft. They render identically under a tiny spilling budget and under a tiny re-decoding budget. That run made 297 evictions, 209 re-materialisations, 69 level rebuilds and 88 spill writes (53 MiB), and lost nothing.

**Numbers:** building the pyramid for 2048² takes 25.6 ms. Evicting with a spill write takes 2.9 ms for 16 MiB, and reading it back takes 1.24 ms (page cache hot). `prepare` adds 3–5 ms to the first walk of bitmap files, on worker threads.

**Gates:** fmt, clippy -D warnings, workspace tests with the corpus (1846 passed, 0 failed, 1 ignored), cargo deny and build --locked: all pass (exit 0).
