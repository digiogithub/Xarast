Round 1 done on branch `worktree-agent-a9c0e9a773d4f239c`. Commits: f0a55c6 (render: banded export rasteriser), d45937c (encoders), e2d23ec (parallel deterministic PNG compression), c7988f0, 6925c74 (docs).

- **T11.2.1**: `xarast_render::export::render_export_strips` and `CpuBackend::render_rows`. The CPU backend is constructed inside the entry, so export cannot reach the GPU. Band and strip heights depend only on the output size (about 64 bands, 64 MiB strips).
- **T11.2.2**: byte-identity holds across runs, strip budgets, 1 vs 8 threads and separate processes. The tests are in `xarast-render/tests/export.rs`, `xarast-io/tests/raster.rs` and the CLI `export_is_byte_identical_across_processes`.
- **T11.2.3**: our own PNG writer. It covers 8/16-bit, RGBA/RGB/grey/grey-alpha/exact palette, Adam7, `pHYs` and `sRGB`. Non-interlaced files stream strip by strip, and DEFLATE runs in parallel in fixed 1 MiB pieces (still deterministic). A 20 000 × 13 694 px export peaks at 372 MB.
- **T11.2.4**: `oxipng` behind the `oxipng` feature, off by default because it builds libdeflate from C. It runs off-thread and is abandoned on cancel.
- **T11.2.5**: JPEG through `jpeg-encoder` (licence `(MIT OR Apache-2.0) AND IJG`; justified in `deny.toml`, acknowledgement tracked as XARA-T-0194). Quality, progressive, 4:4:4/4:2:2/4:2:0, JFIF dpi and EXIF ColorSpace=1 are supported, and the image is always rendered onto an opaque background.
- **T11.2.6**: WebP lossless through `image-webp`. Lossy is refused because no pure-Rust encoder exists.

**Corpus**: all 59 files export to PNG, JPEG and WebP at 96 and 300 dpi with 0 errors. Two runs are byte-identical per format.

**Budgets** (A4 at 300 dpi, 10k objects): PNG 127 ms (budget 3 s), JPEG 132 ms (2.5 s), WebP 147 ms (4 s).

**XARA-T-0038**: the parallelism half is fixed for export, and I commented there. The task stays open for the headless path and the per-pixel cost.
