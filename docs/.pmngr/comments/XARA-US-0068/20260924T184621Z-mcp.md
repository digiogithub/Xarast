**Design.**
- *Render (B).* `blur.rs`: a disc kernel centred on a pixel plus an integer Gaussian, σ = r/2, a 100 px ceiling, soft erosion, a profile table. Exact integer arithmetic that does not depend on the thread count. `layer.rs`: `LayerTarget`/`LayerContent::{Colour, Alpha, Silhouette}`/`render_subtree_to_layer`, tile-aware and cancellable. `effect.rs` + `SceneBuilder::push_effect`: the CPU backend renders each effect's device-space region before the bands and composites it.
- *Exactness under repaint (invariant 23).* The coverage origin is fixed by the view. The region reaches past the kept pixels and past the viewport. The display-list clip grows by the effect's reach. `scene_damage` pads changes under an effect by its reach. The mutation check on that pad fails within 2 000 cases.
- *Doc (A).* Regeneration lives outside the tree: `regen::regenerate` is pure, `LiveCache` is keyed by `RegenKey`, and `RegenQueue` marks entries from the change journal and flushes deepest first. So one change is one undo step, and the double invalidation is the scene diff. `in_generated` now blocks every edit inside generated data.
- *Named decisions.* Blend steps are generated, not materialised. `MouldGeometry` is a trait over a closed enum. The `ProceduralSource::cache_key` covers the parameters and the generator version.
